Splice is played in turns on a board of hexagons, by two or three players. Each
starts with two or three pieces, and the player with the most pieces at the end
wins.

## A move

On your turn, pick one of your pieces and an empty cell near it:

- **Grow** into a cell next to it: a new piece appears there, and the piece you
  picked stays where it is.
- **Jump** to a cell two steps away: the piece moves there and leaves its old
  cell empty. A jump goes over anything in between, holes included.

Then every other player's piece next to the cell you moved into becomes yours.

Magenta moves first, then Cyan, then Amber. A player who has no move (no empty
cell within two steps of any of their pieces) misses their turn.

## The end

- The game ends when at most one player can still move. The empty cells left
  become that player's pieces. So a full board ends it, and so does a player
  losing every piece.
- The player with the most pieces wins. Players with as many pieces share
  their place.
- A turn takes as long as its player thinks, and the match clock stops at half
  an hour. If it does, the player with the most pieces wins.

## Strategy

Growing gains a piece; jumping doesn't, and leaves a hole that an opponent can
grow into, next to your pieces. A piece next to an empty cell can be won over by
the next player, so the strongest moves often fill a gap rather than win the
most pieces now.
